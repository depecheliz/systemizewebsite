Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

This page explains what happens to information submitted through systemizemethod.com. It is general website language, not a substitute for attorney-reviewed legal documentation.

Information collected

The project inquiry form on this site may collect:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Company or brand
  • Website
  • Project type
  • Project description
  • Timeline
  • Optional budget range

Use of information

Information is used to:

  • Review the inquiry
  • Respond to the sender
  • Determine whether Systemize may be a fit

Submitted information is not sold or shared with third parties for marketing purposes.

Service providers

Form submissions may be processed by the website hosting and form-delivery providers used to operate this site (for example, Netlify) in order to deliver inquiries to Systemize.

Retention

Information may be retained for as long as needed to respond to the inquiry, maintain business records, and meet legal obligations.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to info@systemizemethod.com.
