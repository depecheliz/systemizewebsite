Aestella Account & Data Deletion

Aestella: Aesthetic Planner gives you control over your account and personal data.

Delete your Aestella account

You may request permanent deletion of your Aestella account and associated personal data at any time.

Option 1: Delete your account in the Aestella app

Open Aestella and sign in to your account. Navigate to your account/settings area and select the available account deletion option. Follow the confirmation steps to submit your deletion request.

Option 2: Request deletion without the app

If you no longer have Aestella installed or cannot access your account, email:

aestellabeauty@gmail.com

Subject:
Aestella Account Deletion Request

Send the request from the email address associated with your Aestella account whenever possible.

Include the email address associated with the Aestella account you want deleted.

For security, we may need to verify that you are the account owner before processing the request.

What is deleted

When an Aestella account deletion request is completed, personal data associated with the account that is no longer required will be deleted from Aestella's systems. Depending on the features the user has used, this may include:

  • Aestella account/profile information
  • Saved Aesthetics Profile and planning information
  • Saved treatment or aesthetics information
  • Aesthetics Passport records
  • User-uploaded images or photos stored by Aestella
  • AI Preview-related user content stored by Aestella, where applicable
  • Other user-generated or saved data associated with the account

Data that may be retained

Certain limited information may be retained when reasonably necessary for legal, security, fraud-prevention, dispute-resolution, accounting, or regulatory obligations.

Information that must be retained for these purposes will be kept only for the period reasonably necessary to satisfy those obligations and then deleted or anonymized where appropriate.

App-store purchase and subscription transaction records may also be maintained by Google Play, Apple, RevenueCat, payment processors, or other service providers according to their own legal and retention requirements.

Delete specific data without deleting your account

You may request deletion of specific Aestella data without deleting your entire account by emailing:

aestellabeauty@gmail.com

Subject:
Aestella Data Deletion Request

Please identify the specific data you would like deleted and provide the email address associated with your Aestella account.

For security, we may need to verify that you are the account owner before processing the request.
